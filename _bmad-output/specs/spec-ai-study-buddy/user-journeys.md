# User journeys

Ida stands in for the Author; Jonas is the Examiner. Screen-level flows are in EXPERIENCE.md, Key Flows.

- **UJ-1 (Must). Lecture to quiz the same evening.** On a laptop, Ida opens Course "Databases", creates Topic "Normalization", accepts the privacy notice the first time, and uploads 32 lecture slides as PDF at Detail level medium. After a progress indicator the Topic shows Summary, Key concepts and Flashcards. Ida takes a 10-Question Practice-mode Quiz, opens one Hint, and every Answer is graded at once. An incorrect Multiple-choice Answer shows the correct option, why the chosen one is wrong, and "page 14" with the quote. Result 7 of 10; Ida takes the Retry round with the three missed Questions. *Edge:* a 70-page compendium is rejected naming the 50-page limit, so Ida uploads one chapter at a time.
- **UJ-2 (Must; phone use needs FR-25, Should). Due today on the bus.** On the phone, Ida opens Due today: 14 cards from two Courses, flips and rates each Again, Hard, Good or Easy; Again cards return before the end. The Review ends with the count and when the next cards are due.
- **UJ-3 (Must; Exam mode Should). Exam preparation.** The Course overview shows Weak topics "Normalization" 45% and "Transactions" 55%. Ida takes a Practice-mode Quiz from both, then an Exam-mode Quiz: 20 Questions from the whole Course, 30 minutes, no Hints or feedback. The result shows score per Topic, and Topic scores update.
- **UJ-4 (Should). Simpler explanation.** "Third normal form" does not click; Ida asks for a Simpler explanation and gets a short, plain version in the slides' language, with its page.
- **UJ-5 (Must). Examiner without a Key.** Jonas clones, copies `.env.example` to `.env` without a Key, and starts the app per the README. The app shows Mock mode. Jonas signs up, uploads the Sample document, sees its Study material with one item removed by the Reference check, takes a Quiz and a Retry round, and does a Review. *Edge:* another PDF is stored with a message that generating Study material needs a Key.
