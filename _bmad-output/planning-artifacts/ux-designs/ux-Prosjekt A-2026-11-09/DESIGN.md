## Brand & Style

AI Study Buddy is a work tool for adult students, from 20 years and up. It looks grown-up, with nothing childish, and puts **oversiktlig** first: a student must always see every fag they have added. The posture sits closer to a good bank app, Yr, Finn or a university's student web than to a learning app. It is calm, nordic and institutional, and it trusts the student. The feel words are «trygghet, ro og oversiktlighet».

The direction is **14 Fagskinnen** (its lineage is in EXPERIENCE.md, Inspiration & Anti-patterns). Three moves carry the identity:

- **The fag rail.** A flat, deep petrol column on the left (≥ 1024 px) lists every fag with its due count and a small mastery meter, on every signed-in page. It is the overview.
- **The study forecast.** Study load reads like a weather forecast: seven columns of real due-card counts, today marked in copper. It shows real data only, never a prediction the app cannot compute.
- **One next-step band per screen.** A deep petrol band right under the title names the one next action, why, and how long. It holds the page's only copper button.

The visual reference is the mockup [`mockups/14-fagskinnen.html`](mockups/14-fagskinnen.html), which holds all 24 screens (P01–P12, D01–D12) in light and dark. It shows composition; this file and EXPERIENCE.md hold the contract.

**What "adult" means here** (binding on every screen):

- No mascots, emoji, illustrations of people or confetti.
- No streaks, points, badges, levels or other gamification.
- No exclamation marks in UI copy.
- No rounded "friendly" display faces (Nunito, Quicksand, Fredoka, Baloo and the like).
- No saturated rainbow palette and nothing pastel-candy.
- The visual direction supplies only type, palette, density and the three signature moves. Navigation, buttons, tables, dialogs and checkboxes are ordinary web components.
- Corner radii, the single accent and tabular figures are set in Shapes, Colors and Typography.

## Typography

One family: **Public Sans** (Google Fonts, weights 400 to 800), falling back to `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif`. It was made for public services: sober, institutional, with clear tabular figures and no rounding or playfulness. `[ASSUMPTION: loading Public Sans from Google Fonts or self-hosting it is an architecture choice]`

| `title` (`title-mobile` below 1024 px) | One per page: «Hjem», the fag name, the tema name, «Fant ikke siden» |

| `score` | The score in result-summary («7 av 10») |

Rules:

- **Tabular figures everywhere** (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), so counts, percentages, dates and the clock never jump.
- Sentence case everywhere; never all caps, never tracked-out labels.
- Weights go from 400 to 800; 750 is for titles and the today value, 800 for today's count in a day cell and in «I dag · 14».
- Body text is 16 px and never smaller. Reading text stops at 68 characters.
- Study material keeps the language of its kilde and uses the same roles.

## Layout & Spacing

The scale is 4 px based, from `{spacing.1}` to `{spacing.16}`. Density is **balanced**: tables and rows, not cards in a grid, with enough air to read a row at a glance.

**The shell.**

- **≥ 1024 px:** a two-column grid. The fag-rail is `{spacing.rail-width}` wide and full height; the workspace to the right holds the page at most `{spacing.workspace-max}` wide, with padding 28 px top and `{spacing.margin-desktop}` sides.
- **Below 1024 px:** the top-bar (`{spacing.top-bar-height}`, `{spacing.top-bar-height-tablet}` from 600 px), then the fag-switcher strip, then the page with `{spacing.margin-mobile}` (`{spacing.margin-tablet}` from 600 px) side padding.
- **Sign in (P01), from 1024 px:** a 440 px petrol pane on the left with «Alle fagene dine i én liste, på hver side.» and a preview of the rail; the form on the right.
- **Sessions** (quiz, cards, review, exam, results): no rail, no top-bar. The session-bar spans the width; the content centers at `{spacing.quiz-max}` (quiz, exam), `{spacing.reading-max}` (cards, review) or `{spacing.result-max}` (results).

**The page.** Title, then the next-step-band (16–18 px above, 22–26 px below), then the content. One title per page.

**The rail at scale.** A fag row is `{spacing.rail-row-height}` high, so eight fag fit above the account on an 800 px tall laptop screen. With more than 10 fag, the fag list scrolls on its own, while Hjem, «I dag» and the account stay fixed.

**Components respond to their own width** (container queries), not only the viewport:

- The fag forecast switches to day columns when its container is ≥ 560 px (`{spacing.forecast-day}` per day), and to the wide layout at ≥ 820 px (`{spacing.forecast-day-wide}`, `{spacing.forecast-unit-wide}` per card, and a 190 px stat column inside the lead cell).
- The tema table stacks below a container width of 600 px, shows two lines per row from 600 px, and becomes a six-column table with a header row from 860 px.
- The tema page splits into the reading column and a 300 px forecast panel from a screen width of 1200 px.

**Touch targets** are at least `{spacing.touch-target}`; checklist rows are 52 px; small buttons drop to 34 px only from 1024 px, where a pointer is assumed.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from tone first: the dark shell against the white workspace, `ground-2` for quiet fills, `line` for edges. Shadows are few and soft, tinted with the petrol ink:

- **Card shadow** on the forecast, the tema table, the tema-forecast-panel and the flashcard: `0 1px 2px rgba(8, 28, 33, .05), 0 4px 14px rgba(8, 28, 33, .05)`.
- **Band shadow:** `0 1px 2px rgba(8, 28, 33, .12), 0 8px 22px rgba(8, 28, 33, .12)`. It lifts the band just enough to be the most visible thing on the page.
- **Pop shadow** on dialogs and menus: `0 2px 4px rgba(8, 28, 33, .08), 0 14px 36px rgba(8, 28, 33, .18)`, over `{colors.scrim}`.
- **Dark theme:** card `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, .3), 0 6px 18px rgba(0, 0, 0, .22)`; pop `0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, .4), 0 18px 44px rgba(0, 0, 0, .55)`; scrim `{colors.scrim-dark}`.

The rail, top-bar and session-bar are flat; they read as a shell by tone alone.

## Shapes

Crisp, soft-cornered rectangles: the shapes of a well-made form, not of a toy.

- `{rounded.md}` (6 px): buttons, inputs, option-rows, rating-buttons, segmented-controls, rail rows, the fag-switcher button.
- `{rounded.lg}` (8 px): panels, the band, the forecast, tables, flashcards, dialogs (a bottom sheet rounds only its top corners).
- `{rounded.sm}` (4 px): practice-tag, chips, key hints, the focus ring.
- `{rounded.bar}` (3 px): the top corners of forecast bars. `{rounded.xs}` (2 px): rail meters and answer slots.
- `{rounded.full}`: the avatar only.

Icons are 2 px stroke line icons, 20 px (16 px and 24 px variants), rounded caps. They label, never decorate.

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Keep copper for "do this now": the band button, the primary button, today | Use copper for headings, warnings, decoration or the weak state |
| Print the number next to every bar, meter and slot | Let a graphic stand without its value |
| Show trend with an arrow and a word in neutral ink | Color trend green or red |
| Use status colors only on grades and errors, always with an icon and a word | Color-code fag, mastery levels or ratings |
| Keep radii 2–8 px; rectangles with soft corners | Pills, bubbles or fully rounded cards (the avatar is the only circle) |
| One next-step-band per page, right under the title | A second band, a band inside a card, or a band with two buttons |
| Put the fag overview in the rail on every signed-in page from 1024 px | Hide the fag list behind a hamburger on desktop |
| Set every number in tabular figures | Proportional digits in counts, dates or the clock |
| Write calm, plain copy without exclamation marks | Mascots, emoji, confetti, streaks, points, badges or levels |
| Keep the dark theme designed: darker rail, lighter copper with dark text | Invert the light theme or use pure black |
