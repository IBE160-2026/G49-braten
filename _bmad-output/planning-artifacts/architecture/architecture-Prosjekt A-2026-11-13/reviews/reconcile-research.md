## Top items to settle before step 1 (20 Nov)

1. **R-01, with C-01 and R-02:** run and record the NFR-5 extraction test on 3 slide decks, using pypdf 7.0.1 (plain and layout modes) against pdfplumber, before pypdf is treated as locked.
2. **R-03:** widen the quote normalisation. Otherwise slide decks will lose valid items to ligatures and reading order.
3. **R-05 and R-08:** constraint-free provider schemas, and an explicit Mock override so tests and demos are safe on a keyed machine.
4. **C-03 and C-04:** latency and rate-limit measurement at the 50-page limit, since both decide whether in-request generation (AD-5, AD-16) holds.
